# Superstore_Sales_Profitability_Analysis_Excel
An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing Superstore sales, profitability, customer segments, regional performance, product categories, and monthly trends using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers.


📊 Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis Dashboard | Excel

📌 Project Overview

The "Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis Dashboard" is an interactive Data Analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel.

This project focuses on analyzing business performance through sales, profit, quantity sold, customer orders, profit margins, and average order value.

Using the Superstore dataset (2014–2017), this project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI Cards, and Interactive Slicers.

The dashboard provides an overview of sales performance across different regions, states, customer segments, product categories, shipping modes, and monthly trends.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Track monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Identify top-performing states and customers.
- Analyze sales performance across different regions.
- Understand customer segment distribution.
- Evaluate product category performance.
- Compare revenue across different shipping modes.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business performance monitoring.

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes six major KPIs to monitor overall business performance:

- Total Sales: Overall revenue generated from sales.
- Total Quantity Sold: Total number of product units sold.
-Total Profit: Overall profit generated from business transactions.
- Profit Margin (%): Percentage of profit relative to total sales.
- Total Orders: Total number of unique customer orders.
- Average Order Value (AOV): Average sales value generated per order.

📊 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes nine interactive visualizations to explore different aspects of business performance:

1. Monthly Revenue Trend: Analyzes monthly sales performance and revenue fluctuations.
2. Monthly Profit Trend: Tracks monthly profitability and profit trends.
3. Revenue by State: Displays geographical sales distribution across different states.
4. Revenue by Region: Compares sales performance across different regions.
5. Top 5 States by Revenue: Identifies the five highest revenue-generating states.
6. Revenue by Customer Segment: Analyzes revenue contribution from different customer segments.
7. Revenue by Ship Mode: Compares revenue across different shipping methods.
8. Revenue by Category: Analyzes sales contribution from different product categories.
9. Top 5 Customers by Revenue: Identifies the five highest revenue-generating customers.

🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Filters

The dashboard includes the following interactive slicers:

- Year (2014–2017)
- Quarter (Q1–Q4)
- Month Name
- Region (Central, East, South, West)
- Segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
- Category (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology)
- Ship Mode (First Class, Same Day, Second Class, Standard Class)

These filters allow users to explore business performance across different time periods and business dimensions.
